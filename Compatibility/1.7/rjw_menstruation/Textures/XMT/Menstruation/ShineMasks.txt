Cryptimorph UI shine masks

Runtime textures:
  Cryptimorph/<relative path>.png        Existing tinted anatomy art
  Masks/Cryptimorph/<relative path>.png  Optional white/transparent highlight
Every runtime cryptimorph PNG has a same-size starter mask. Shared milk graphics,
additive fluid overlays (including their empty placeholder), and source ~ templates
are excluded. Empty masks are allowed.

Starter construction selects the highest HSV value (max RGB) among nontransparent
pixels in each texture. Each 8-connected plateau is eroded by approximately 5% of
its shorter span at each edge, producing about 10% total linear shrink. The radius
rounds to whole pixels; tiny plateaus can remain unchanged and thin areas may
disappear. Transparent pixels do not establish the maximum. Black has no shine.
The result is white RGB with the retained source alpha; no base pixels are edited.

Breasts.ora, Genitals.ora, Womb_and_Fetus.ora and Egg_and_Ovary.ora have been
regenerated with a hidden top-level group named:
  Cryptimorph shine masks - export individually
It mirrors the art group hierarchy, with matching layer names, sizes and positions.
Use these groups to edit individual masks. White restores shine; transparent or
black removes it; gray/partial alpha gives weaker shine. The exporter normalizes
gray intensity into the alpha channel and writes white RGB.

Existing KRAs were NOT overwritten. Transfer the named mask group from the matching
new ORA into your edited KRA, retaining its names and hierarchy. Without that group,
existing KRAs can still export their art and preserve the current mask PNGs on disk.
Fetus.kra is unchanged and remains a manual six-stage workflow; its corresponding
cryptimorph masks are editable in Womb_and_Fetus.ora.

The Export_*.py scripts include associated masks for selected cryptimorph textures.
Their include/exclude patterns still match the ART destination, so the breast
nipple/areola-only preset exports those same nipple/areola masks automatically.
Set EXPORT_SHINE_MASKS = False to export art without replacing masks.
Masks are read from their own layer/group projection: global ColorHue/treatment
layers are not baked into masks. Attach mask-specific filters to a mask layer, or
replace that layer with a same-named group containing the mask and its treatment.
The open document's pixels, selection and visibility remain untouched.

The runtime keeps the original Menstruation draw, then draws the matching highlight
over it with a neutral tint. It preserves UI positions, scaling, UVs, rotation and
GUI color. Only cryptimorph-namespaced textures can use these masks. Ordinary pawns
and missing/differently-sized masks use the existing draw without a highlight pass.
This is a fixed painted shine effect; it does not respond to world lights.

Defs/CryptimorphTextures.xml (relative to this compatibility mod):
  shineMaskPrefix = XMT/Menstruation/Masks/Cryptimorph/
  shineOpacity = 0.65  (0 disables highlights; values are clamped to 0..1)

Tools/GenerateShineMasks.py creates missing masks from current runtime art.
It preserves existing masks; --overwrite discards their manual edits.
Tools/BuildRegionDocuments.py --overwrite recreates the ORAs and their mask groups;
it never rewrites KRAs. Tools/BuildKritaExporters.py regenerates export scripts.

Validation: all 464 masks checked for matching dimensions and alpha bounds;
generation checked for maximum selection and 10% erosion. Installed-binary audit
confirms all 13 draw calls across five upstream UI methods have compatible wrappers
and Harmony patches apply. Live RimWorld/Krita visual verification remains.
